# Escalation Workflow

## 1. Understand
Read the complete conversation and identify the guest/client's main concern.

## 2. Verify
Check the relevant booking, property, account, or operational information.

## 3. Prioritize
Determine whether the issue affects safety, access, active stays, revenue, or normal operations.

## 4. Resolve or Coordinate
Handle what can be resolved directly. Escalate to the correct team when another department or vendor is required.

## 5. Communicate
Give clear expectations and avoid promising something that has not been confirmed.

## 6. Document
Record actions taken, important details, and the outcome.

## 7. Follow Up
Confirm the issue has actually been resolved.
